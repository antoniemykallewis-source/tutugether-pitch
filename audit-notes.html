What each label means

Observed: a visible page or public HTTP response. Possible impact: a reason to test a fix, not measured lost revenue. Unavailable: a check we could not verify.

Website crawl and SEO

All 25 URLs from the WordPress page sitemap were fetched. Homepage-linked URLs, two product pages, robots.txt and sitemap routes were also checked. This does not cover every asset, generated archive, language or third-party endpoint. The table lists HTTP responses, not an assurance that each page works fully. The checkout link redirected to the cart with no purchase made.

The public HTML included Google tag GT-NBQGQHQW. No Meta Pixel marker was found in the sampled HTML. This does not rule out scripts loaded later, consent-dependent tracking, server events or tags in external booking steps. Event firing and analytics access were not verified. No tracking claim is presented as a defect in the deck.

We read titles, H1 headings, meta descriptions and robots directives, and checked for structured-data blocks. A broader technical SEO audit and Search Console inspection remain open. The screenshot-backed SEO issue is the broken sitemap address in robots.txt. The working WordPress sitemap does not prove that Google has read it.

Conversion and location

Observed route: homepage Book Now opens Santa Clara. The Sacramento link switches to Roseville, then Begin Roseville opens the event choice, then Regular Reservation opens Project 1 of 4. That is four navigation choices before project selection when starting through the default homepage route, excluding scrolls. A direct Roseville footer link avoids the Santa Clara detour. We stopped before customer information and payment, so the complete booking tap count is unavailable.

The Roseville booking and contact pages have a tel: link for 916-474-4003. The footer displays phone numbers as text. No call was placed. Mobile above-the-fold and touch-size testing of the business site was not completed.

Speed

PageSpeed Insights hit Google's unusual-traffic challenge before a report could run. Mobile and desktop performance scores, largest-contentful-paint timing and total page weight are unavailable. HTML bytes in our private crawl are not page weight and are not used as a substitute.

Social, ads and Google

Instagram displayed 6,597 followers and recent creator reels dated November 6, November 2 and late October. These dates were read from the visible page labels, not captured in the profile screenshot. They are background notes, not a screenshot-backed finding. A pinned January 2025 post was not treated as the newest post. Engagement rate, full posting frequency and bookings from creators remain unavailable. The linked TikTok address returned an account-not-found screen.

Meta's U.S. active-ad keyword search returned no matches for tutugether. Advertiser-page identity was not verified, so active-ad count, age, creative type and landing-page quality are not claimed. Facebook page activity was not verified.

The public business search surfaced a Roseville listing at the same address and phone shown on the site, but a complete Google Business Profile screen review was not finished. Rating, review count, recency, replies, photos, categories and website-button destination were not used as findings. Three location-controlled searches remain open: rug tufting Roseville, craft workshops Roseville, and birthday craft parties Roseville. No local-rank position is claimed.
