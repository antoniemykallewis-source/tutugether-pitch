What you do well

What we found / 1 of 3

First clicks.Clear paths.

01

Book Now starts in San Jose.

The home page’s Book Now button opens the Valley Fair / Santa Clara form. Roseville visitors must choose a different location.

Our fix. Start with a clear studio choice. Send Roseville ads straight to its booking page.

The form loaded. We did not book or measure lost bookings.

02

The TikTok link hits a dead end.

The site links to @tutugether.ca. TikTok showed “Couldn’t find this account” when we opened that exact link.

Our fix. Confirm the right handle. Update the site’s link and test it while signed out.

Seen signed out on Nov 6. This does not prove there is no account.

What we found / 2 of 3

Two broken pages.Simple fixes.

03

The search map points to a 404.

The robots.txt file points search engines to sitemap_indexs.xml. That link shows “Page not found.”

Our fix. Use the working WordPress page map. Keep test and payment-result pages out of it.

A working page map exists. We did not measure any ranking effect.

04

About us opens a missing page.

The home page and footer link to /about/. That page returns a 404 error, so visitors miss the studio’s story.

Our fix. Restore the page or fix the links. Show the team and what a first visit is like.

We checked the error page and its 404 status. Click counts are unknown.

What we found / 3 of 3

Studio details.Group plans.

05

The SF pages tell two stories.

The SF project page says “Coming soon.” The contact page still lists an SF address and hours. Booking also lists SF.

Our fix. Confirm the studio’s status, then give each page the same clear message.

This is a content mismatch. It does not prove the studio is closed.

06

Mixed craft groups book separately.

Roseville asks groups to book tufting and decoden/pouring separately. Groups larger than the online openings must email.

Our fix. Offer one group request with craft choices and a clear reply time.

This is the stated policy, not a broken form. We did not submit it.

Show its crafts, address, hours, parking and booking link. Confirm SF’s status. Repair the sitemap route and keep unfinished pages out of search.
